The 2026 DGIST AI Build Week “AI Hackathon!”

July 24, 2026

On July 24, 2026, I joined the DGIST AI Build Week hackathon, an intensive event centered on rapidly turning AI ideas into working prototypes.

The experience brought together participants with different technical backgrounds and highlighted the value of fast iteration, clear problem framing, and collaborative engineering. It was a rewarding opportunity to build, test, exchange ideas, and learn from other teams.

Events like this are a reminder that meaningful AI development is not only about models and code. It also depends on teamwork, communication, and the ability to connect technical tools to real problems.
